Ecclesiastes 3:1-8 "For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter 
under heaven. This is a reminder that our lives are constantly changing, and that's 
something we should expect. Everything we go through, whether good or bad, has a 
purpose, even if we don't understand it at the time. It may be to deepen our faith, teach 
us a valuable lesson, or help us grow as a person. There are seasons of seedtime, waiting 
and harvest… seasons of testing and breakthrough. The difficult seasons are necessary 
for the good times to come.  life isn't so simple as fearing God and choosing wisdom. Life 
is fleeting and unpredictable and our existence is a blip of time.  
The Bible can help us during tough times. It reminds us that God loves us, is 
powerful, and keeps His promises. If you are facing a crisis, feeling overwhelmed, 
hopeless, difficult situation and lost, read the Bible verses above and others that speak 
to your heart. Also, pray to God for help and guidance. He is always there for us. 
Remember, God's Word is living and active. It is not just a book to be read, it is a powerful 
tool that can transform our lives. When we spend time in the Bible, we are connecting 
with God and allowing Him to speak to our hearts. To fall in stay with God’s words amidst 
the proliferation of fake news and continuing disinformation in society, be intentional in 
our Bible reading, don’t just read it passively, take time to reflect and apply it in your own 
life. Talk to others. Talk to other Christians about what you're learning in the Bible. Discuss 

your questions and doubts. This can help you to better understand God's Word and to 
grow in your faith. 
Don't be discouraged by the proliferation of fake news and disinformation. Trust 
that God's Word is true and that it will help you to find the truth in all areas of your life. 
There’s nothing impossible with God.
