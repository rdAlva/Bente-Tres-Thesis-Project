Angeles City 
 
Abstract 
The research aimed to determine the response of the SME’s in the Metro Clark Area on their marketing 
strategy and enterprise success using Social Media platforms as a business tool. Various activities were 
examined through correlation analysis to have a better insight on social media’s impact to the SMEs. The 
study had set a confidence level of 95% and a level of significance of 9% to determine the sample population 
of the study, Cronbach’s Alpha was used in reliability scale. Simple random sampling was used in 
identifying the respondent. Likewise, convenience data gathering was employed based on accessibility, 
availability and cost effectiveness of gathering data. SPPS was utilized to analyze the data. The results had 
significantly shown that the availability of social media application ready devices did not create a barrier 
nor internet connectivity an obstacle on the other hand a higher degree of participation in social media is 
encouraged. The promotion of social media for various marketing strategies and activities cannot be 
overlooked, hence, there should be a greater promotion and engagement.  Create the right and a responsive 
awareness campaign that needs support from all sectors, primarily the government to encourage and 
realized the effect of social media in achieving enterprise success resulting in a better financial performance. 
The study concludes that a policy guideline aimed at protecting the SMEs from unethical and deceitful 
users of social media. Furthermore, an incentive scheme for SMEs needs to be studied to encourage usage 
of social media tools and applications for business endeavors.

Social media as stated by Kaplan and Haenlin (2010) had evolved from the used of internet which 
had became an avenue for exchanging information from among users. It was further noted that the 
Bulletin Board System of the 1980s had paved way for the social media networking. The BBS 
works as a platform for sharing software and data and also end private messages to users through 
posting in a public message boards.  
Social Media also known as social networking is a phenomenon that changes the business 
environment which also includes Web 2.0 where it creates network of communities through 
collaboration of producing and sharing media content. In such case, users were able to share and 
their views and experiences using the social media platform. The effect is a tangible process that 
enhances creativity, communication and knowledge sharing while using social media tools like 
Facebook, Skype and various discussion boards and platforms, (Tapscott and Williams, 2008) 
The Social Media had paved way for business entities to explore possibilities outside their 
frontiers. Zontanos and Anderson (2004) noted that social networking allows businesses to 
established their worthiness, and forged tie up and strategic alliances and dynamism. The 
contribution of social media is networking between players in the industry benefitting business 
owners who does not have adequate technical skills, attitude and abilities and the much needed 
knowledge to be competitive in the market and moreover, attracting qualified manpower for much 

needed expansion (Simon, 2012). He further noted that possibilities are numerous and 
unprecedented with the introduction of social media, tremendous benefits can be realized. Social 
networking platforms had opened the doors for greater access to potential target market, providing 
and effective customer relations management, quality products and services that satisfy the 
customers and instituting a favourable pricing mechanism for all sectors involved (Simon, 2012).  
Smith and Taylor (2004) stated that firms on the 21st century had faced massive changes as they

Research Methodology 
The study will utilize descriptive research design to know the extent on the use of social media 
and its impact marketing activities and SMEs growth in Metro Clark Area. The study will focus 
on SMEs that are registered business entity classified as small and medium enterprise. Simple 
random sampling will be utilized to derive the desired target respondents. Purposive sampling will 
be used to get an easy access on the needed sample size.  A structured questionnaire will be 
administered from owners and/or managers of the SMEs. Closed questions and open-ended 
questions of questionnaires will be utilized to assure reliable and complete data generated. 
Analysis of data will be done through SPSS (Statistical Package for Social Sciences) to calculate 
and determine descriptive statistics and correlation analysis. Descriptive statistical analysis will be 
generated and data will be tabulated using percentages for the nonnumeric information gathered 
where the findings were summarized, explained and interpreted.  
 
Data Collection (Primary and Secondary) 

The primary data will comprise of the data from the interview conducted and questionnaires 
distributed to managers, owner and key employees of the identified respondent firms. Interview 
with experts on social media usage from the IT sector, media practitioners, academics, business 
representative, communication facility service providers and government authorities will also be 
considered.  
The secondary data that will be utilized in this study will be taken from journals and articles, 
website materials, government portal and reference books where they provide the much needed 
data for the conduct of the study. 
 
Sampling Technique  
The researcher will use descriptive.  Both primary and secondary data will be used, particularly 
survey questionnaire and other published and unpublished study related to this topic. The pre-
tested questionnaires will be distributed to identified respondents of the study. Likert scale will be 
used to determine the degree of response of the respondents. The questionnaires will be provided 
to respondents and will be picked up on an agreed time. The research enumerator will explain what 
the study all about and how the questionnaire will be filled out by the respondents.   
 
Sampling Size of the Study 
The survey questionnaires will be randomly distributed to SMEs available at Metro Clark Area 
based on the researchers’ convenience and data will be analyzed with the aid of SPSS (Statistical 
Package for Social Sciences 
 
Data Gathering 
The questionnaire will be designed to gather data in determining the marketing activities and 
growth of SMEs in the Metro Clark Area as an impact on the usage of social media. To support 
the study; the researcher will make follow up interview with respondents in validating and 
clarifying the data gathered through the questionnaire. Questionnaires will be pre tested and be 
validated its reliability using Cronbach’s Alpha reliability scale.
