1. After watching the video, summarize the major ethical concerns surrounding Artificial 
Intelligence (AI). 
The three main ethical concerns surrounding Artificial Intelligence (AI) are the potential misuse 
of AI, limited access to AI development, and AI’s lack of shared human values. We must ensure 
that the AI we create benefits people and consider how this technology might have a negative 
impact on us. Because running AI requires high resources, big companies control our access to 
this data, restricting how AI will be used in the future. AI will perform tasks as instructed, but it is 
a machine that does not share our values and could take misguided actions. This raises ethical 
questions about the risks AI could pose to us.
