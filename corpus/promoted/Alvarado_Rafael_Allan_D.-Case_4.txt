Answer the following: 
1.​ Do you think that Facebook or careless, uninformed users should be held responsible for privacy 
issues related to using Facebook? Explain. 
2.​ What additional measures should Facebook take to protect user privacy? What additional 
actions are required on the part of Facebook users to maintain adequate privacy? 
3.​ Describe a privacy issue so serious that it would cause you to stop using Facebook. 
 
I believe both Facebook and users are responsible for privacy issues on Facebook. Why do I say both are 
responsible, it is because before we create our account on Facebook, we have to accept the terms and 
conditions for us to use Facebook. A lot of users tend to just agree on terms and conditions without even 
reading it. I believe Facebook is using ethical egoism in this situation because it gives our information as 
users to their clients such as advertisers for their profit. I think that the executives of Facebook should 
think about their clients or their users more than their benefits. They should let go of their ego serve 
their users with good service and provide them with excellent security on their data. I think the actions 
that Facebook should take is to fix and update their privacy policy. They should make it tougher for 
people with malicious minds to create accounts for them to scam other Facebook users. They should 
make excellent security on their users' accounts to lessen account hacking. I think the privacy issues that 
would make me stop using Facebook are when I see something that I post or when my personal 
information is being sold and published without my permission as it is a breach of the data privacy act of 
the Philippines. At the same time even if I sue Facebook for selling and using my information without my 
knowledge and permission, I don’t think I will win with how much money Facebook has. Every user 
should know their rights and they should read the terms and conditions before accepting it.
