“Myself and the Solar System” 
 
I am simply a speck of dust that was formed by a Goldilocks condition from my parents. 
My place in this vast universe is me being a good son to my parents. What keeps me going is my 
dream of being able to finally support my family financially. Having the right amount of time to 
study and the right amount of spending time with my family and friends. Having the right balance 
or condition in my everyday life keeps me going like how there were the right conditions when 
the universe began. I think that this is all heading to me being a better person, making good 
decisions in my life, and having the knowledge that will help me in the future. Currently, in my 1st 
year of college, I am expanding my knowledge bit by bit like how the universe spent a lot of time 
before planets began to form. I think that the reason that I am here is for me to help my family live 
a better life when I get a job in the future. I want a future where my parents can be at ease about 
any financial needs and where we are still complete and a happy family.
