Abstract 
 
The purpose of the study is to determine the relationship of learning 
choice tactics and positive reinforcement on M.A.C brand. The study used 
Raosoft calculator to compute the total population and came up with the 
sample size of 306 respondents. A set of self-made questionnaires was 
administered based on the factors of learning choice tactics. Price tactic 
and Variety Seeking tactic got the highest overall mean rating indicating 
that their customers buying behavior is based on price and they need to 
try something new. While the factor Habit tactic got the lowest mean 
rating, the study also shows that there is a Weak Positive Correlation 
between Choice tactics and Positive Reinforcement. This means that the 
brand M.A.C is not as popular like other brands. Pearson’s Correlation 
result showed that Brand Loyalty tactic gained the highest with the 
analysis of “Weak Positive Correlation” impact to Positive Reinforcement 
on M.A.C brand. However, the brand itself is less exposed to other people. 
Therefore, the researchers recommend focusing on customers type based 
on their skin type and this feature of a person which is more a sensitive 
problem. To get the reliability and stability, they need to focus on the real 
content of each product, or the ingredients of product and get the 
similarities based on the quality customer service, loyalty card and efforts 
that will result to more brand exposure and drive more revenue as a result. 
 
 
Key Terms: Learning Choice, Positive Reinforcement, Price Tactic, 
Variety Seeking Tactic, Habit Tactic, Brand Loyalty Tactic.

interpreted as "Strongly Agee".

grand mean of 3.26 among other factors which were verbally interpreted as "strongly agree".

mean rating of 3.29, while the statement "I often buy M.A.C products weekly or monthly" 
obtained the mean rating of 3.24 with descriptive rating of agree.

Learning choice tactics to positive reinforcement with a Pearson’s r of 0.475 and p-value of 
0.001 which were interpreted as Weak positive correlation. While the Habit tactic gained the 
weakest correlated factors to positive reinforcement with a Pearson’s r of 0.244 and p-value of 
0.001 which were interpreted as Weak positive correlation.

                                        Definition of Terms 
Choice Tactics – Simple rules of thumbs used to make low-effort decisions. 
Price Tactic – Simplifying decision heuristics that are based on price. 
Affect Tactic – Tactics based on feelings. 
Performance-related Tactic – Tactics based on benefits, features, or evaluation of the brand. 
Normative Tactic – Low-elaboration decision making that is based on others’ opinions. 
Habit Tactic – Doing the same thing time after time. 
Brand Loyalty Tactic – Buying the same brand repeatedly because of a strong preference for 
it. 
Variety Seeking Tactic – Trying something different. 
Positive Reinforcement – Generally originates from a sentiment of fulfillment that happens 
when we as purchasers see that our needs have been satisfactorily met.
