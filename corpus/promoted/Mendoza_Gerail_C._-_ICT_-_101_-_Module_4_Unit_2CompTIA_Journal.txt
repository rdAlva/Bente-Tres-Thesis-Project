1.  Ethernet switch. - It is a central hub that allows multiple devices to communicate with 
each other by forwarding data packets between them. They are typically used in both 
home and business networks, and they come in a variety of sizes and configurations to 
meet the needs of different environments. 
 
2.  Your home network and your internet service provider (ISP) are connected via a 
modem. It transforms your ISP's signals into a language that your devices can 
understand. Coaxial cables or phone lines are the two common ways that modems are 
connected. While A router is a device that shares your modem's internet connection with 
every device connected to your home network. Additionally, it enables inter-device 
communication on your network.
