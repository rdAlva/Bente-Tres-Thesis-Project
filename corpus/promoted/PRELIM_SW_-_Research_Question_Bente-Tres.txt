Research Question​
 What is the relationship between the level of government corruption and the effectiveness of 
public service delivery in the Philippines? 
Justification​
 Corruption is a persistent and significant issue in the Philippines, affecting governance, 
economic development, and public trust in institutions. Examining its impact on public 
service delivery is highly relevant because services such as healthcare, education, and 
infrastructure directly influence citizens’ quality of life. A quantitative analysis can provide 
empirical evidence on how corruption levels relate to service effectiveness, which can inform 
policy reforms, anti-corruption strategies, and accountability mechanisms aimed at improving 
government performance. 
Independent and Dependent Variables​
 The independent variable is the level of government corruption, which can be measured 
using indicators such as corruption perception scores, reported corruption cases, or audit 
findings across government agencies or regions. The dependent variable is the 
effectiveness of public service delivery, which can be measured through quantitative 
indicators such as service completion rates, budget utilisation efficiency, infrastructure 
project completion times, or citizen satisfaction survey scores. Analysing these variables 
allows for measurable comparisons and identification of patterns between corruption levels 
and service outcomes.
