Trojan Horse - A Trojan horse is a type of malware that disguises itself as legitimate software. 
Once installed on a computer, a Trojan horse can perform a variety of malicious Trojan horses are 
often spread through phishing emails or malicious websites. When a user clicks on a malicious 
link or opens an infected attachment, the Trojan horse is installed on their computer without their 
knowledge. actions, such as stealing data, installing other malware, or disrupting system 
operations. there are some signs that may indicate that a computer is infected with a Trojan horse, 
like Slow computer performance, unexpected pop-up ads, Frequent crashes, or freezes, 
Disappearing files, Changes to your system settings. 
Adware - Adware is unwanted software that displays advertisements on the user's computer. 
Adware can be bundled with other software, downloaded from malicious websites, or installed 
through drive-by downloads. Adware is not as dangerous as some other types of malwares, but it 
can be annoying and intrusive. Adware can also slow down your computer and make it difficult to 
browse the web.  
Spyware - Spyware is software that collects information about the user's computer activity without 
their knowledge or consent. Spyware can track the user's browsing history, keystrokes, and 
passwords. It can also steal personal information, such as credit card numbers and social security 
numbers. Spyware is often spread through malicious websites or phishing emails. When a user 
clicks on a malicious link or opens an infected attachment, the spyware is installed on their 
computer without their knowledge. However, there are some signs that may indicate that a 
computer is infected with spyware, such as, Slow computer performance, unexpected pop-up ads, 
Frequent crashes or freezes, Changes to your browser settings, Disappearing files, Unexplained 
internet activity. 
 
2.  Keep your software up to date, use a firewall and Anti-virus software, be careful on links and 
attachments you open, Download software from trusted sources only. Be cautious about what you 
share online, use strong passwords, and always use HTTPS.
