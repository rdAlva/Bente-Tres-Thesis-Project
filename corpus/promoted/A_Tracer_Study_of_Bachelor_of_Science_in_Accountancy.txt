A Tracer Study of Bachelor of Science in Accountancy (BSA) Graduates from 
2014 to 2019, Systems Plus College Foundation 
 Mr. Gian Carlo N. Catahum 
 
 
Abstract:  
 
The study is a mechanism to determine the gap between what the educational institution 
produce and what the industry needs with respect to the evaluation and satisfaction level 
of the graduates. The study was conducted to determine the employability of the BSA 
graduates of SPCF from 2014 to 2019. This also aimed to determine the relevance of the 
curriculum and study program, the practices at BSA and the competencies that helped 
the graduates land a job and how they are useful in their jobs. 
 
There were 94 graduates that participated on the study out of the 103 graduates from 
2014-2019. Questionnaire was administered to the respondents by students, and faculty 
staff of BSBA Department. The data gathering was a concerted effort of alumni, faculty 
staff, students and student leaders to the extent of using social media to reach out to the 
target respondents. The data generated were analyzed and interpreted using simple 
statistical tool as percentages and results were presented in tables. 
 
It found out in the study that 89.4% of the respondents gained employment. Significantly, 
sizable number of graduates acknowledged that curriculum, study program and practices 
at BSBA were useful in their jobs. It was also established that referral system is still a 
reliable and effective way of landing a job for the graduates. 
 
It was also find out that for the graduates to be more competitive and employable the 
following areas needs to be strengthened as follows: entrepreneurial skills, 
communication skills, problem and decision making skills, language proficiency and  
information communication technologies (ICT) skills. It is highly recommended also to 
institutionalize further the establishment of linkage to industry partners in Angeles City 
and Pampanga.

Chapter 1 
 
Introduction 
 
SPCF envisions itself to become a leading and globally competitive educational institution 
in the fields of instruction, research and community service. The study enlightened the 
school authorities and its faculty and staff through knowing the present employability of 
its graduates at the BSA Department. 
 
The findings of the study were an important tool and resource material for the institution 
and its students and graduates. The study mapped out the path towards alignment of 
course offerings, crafting strategies and methodologies that suit to the needs of the 
industry given the ever-changing and highly volatile needs and specifications of the job 
market. This also been an eye-opener for the school authorities in the provision of needed 
resources and services for the students to more competitive in an increasingly 
technological and global labor market competition.

Chapter 2 
 
Problem of the Study 
 
What is the employability rate of SPCF BSA graduates of 2014-2019? 
 
This study aimed to provide facts and information intended to make the institution realize 
the employability rate of BSA graduates from 2014 to 2019. This study further determined 
the effectiveness of the BSA programs, curriculum and the methodologies as well as the 
whereabouts and the present situation of the graduates. The findings of this study can 
help the institution in its curriculum development and review to upgrade the knowledge 
skill and abilities needed by its students in consonance with requirements of the industry, 
or the labor market, thus assuring the graduates of higher chance of employment. 
 
Objectives: 
 
The study aimed to: 
 
a. determine the employment/unemployment rate of SPCF graduates; 
b. ascertain the reasons for graduates’ unemployment; 
c. determine how long graduates could land a job after graduation; 
d. determine the sources for finding a job; 
e. discover the reasons for a graduates’ staying on the job; 
f. ascertain the competencies learned in college, which graduates found very useful; 
and 
g. determine which BA related activities they practiced in their jobs.

Chapter 3 
 
Research Methodology 
 
Sampling Plan  
 
The respondents of the study comprised mainly of SPCF, BSA graduates from 2014 to 
2019. 
 
Sampling Size 
 
There were 112 graduates from the BSA Department from 2014-2019 where all graduates 
were considered as the research sample; hence, total enumeration was employed.  
 
Research Instrument and Technique 
 
A survey questionnaire was devised to gather vital information for the study. The 
Questionnaire had been designed in a way the respondents could easily understand what 
is being asked.  
 
Data Gathering Procedure 
 
The researcher sought the help of the school administration, the registrar’s office, Office 
of external affairs, research and development office, Alumni Office, COB Dean’s Office 
and Student organization who had established network or linkage with the graduates of 
the BSA Department. 
 
The faculty members of the BSA Department, the researcher sought cooperation of the 
students, who served as research enumerators for the study. The researcher had 
selected presently enrolled students and members of the student organization of the 
department to help in the generation of needed data. 
 
Administration of research instruments was given from the start of First Semester, School 
Year 2019-2020 upon pre-testing and validation of materials. The date of retrieval was at 
the end of August to come up with the final result and the presentation was before the 
end of the First Semester, SY 2019-2020. 
 
 

5 
 
 
Statistical Treatment 
 
The data generated were tallied, tabulated, analysed and treated statistically. 
 
Results were treated through frequency, percentages and presented using tables and pie 
charts.

Chapter 5 
 
Conclusion and Recommendation 
 
It can be noted that from among the 94 respondents that accounts to 83% of the total 
BSBA graduates from 2011 to 2014, there were 89.4% or 84 graduates had gained 
employment or currently working. Notably, 66% are still connected with their first job 
which explains that there is a high level of satisfaction on their current jobs.  
 
More than 52% reported that they land a job within six months after graduation and 
almost 30% got a job before one year. Hence, employability of the graduates was very 
high. It was acknowledged by the graduates that referral from people related from SPCF 
and to SPCF itself had greatly been the reason why they were employed. Job fair and 
walk in application was also widely acknowledged. 
 
The reason on unemployment still perceived by handful respondents as they lack 
connections and they lack experience and still contested that there was no job 
opportunity. 

39 
 
 
It found out that the BSBA curriculum was acknowledged by 93.6% as relevant to their 
job. As they also stated that the competencies they gained from BSBA were useful in 
their job namely: customer care and customer relations, problem solving and decision 
making skills and communication skills. It also found out that activities gained from 
BSBA were practiced at their job like marketing activities, customer relations, data 
gathering and research, and community extension activities. 
 
Currently, most graduates are working in Angeles City and in Pampanga. The 
graduates acknowledge that they are highly adaptable in their jobs. It was found out 
also that graduates gained experiences from their current job and shared that their 
study program was relevant to their job. 
 
On the other hand, the following areas should be strengthened for the BSBA  graduates 
to be more competitive and employable in the future, these are: entrepreneurial skills, 
communication skills, problem and decision making skills, language proficiency and ICT 
skills. It is further recommended that linkages to industries or companies within Angeles 
City and Pampanga should be established as they are the major labor market for BSBA 
Graduates.
