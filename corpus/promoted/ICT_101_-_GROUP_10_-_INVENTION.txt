What is your Invention? what does it do?  
 
As we all know, charcoal is an excellent fuel, but it does affect our environment by cutting 
down so many logs of trees. We all know that dry leaves go to waste or drainage which affects our 
environment, and we did not seem to care. We invented alternative charcoal using dry leaves, 
which can lessen of using logs from trees that could have potential benefits, especially in terms of 
utilizing a readily available and renewable resource. 
 
Yes, dried leaves can be used as charcoal fuel. In fact, they are a good source of renewable 
energy and can help to reduce deforestation. These are a sustainable and affordable way to cook, 
and they are also less smoky than traditional fuels, which can help to reduce indoor air pollution. 
 
How is your invention similar to or different from existing tools or technologies in terms of 
function?  
 
Charcoal made with leaves is different from existing tools in terms of ingredients because 
our charcoal is easy to make, and easier to find. It is also a product that is eco-friendly to the 
environment plus the cost material is budget friendly. 
 
Charcoal made with leaves is a more sustainable and less smoky fuel than wood or coal. It 
is also comparable to natural gas and electricity in terms of heat output. However, it is important 
to note that the environmental impact of charcoal made with leaves will also depend on how the 
leaves are collected and processed.
