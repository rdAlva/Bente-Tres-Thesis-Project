1. How does the video define/describe "software architecture" versus "software design"? 
Software architecture refers to the overall structure of a software system. It involves identifying 
the business requirements for the project and understanding how the different components of the 
software interact with each other. In contrast, software design focuses on the detailed planning 
and implementation of individual components. It outlines how specific features and 
functionalities will be developed within the software. 
2. Explain how your project's software architecture guides your design decisions.  
In our project, Vendwise, we have decided to adopt a mobile-based approach for our core 
structure. We will utilize Flutter for our frontend development, Spring Boot for our backend, and 
SQLite for our database. This decision will shape the overall architecture of our system and 
define how each component interacts with the others. By establishing a solid software 
architecture for our application, we can enhance the efficiency of its processes. Given our focus 
on a mobile-based approach, we can also take advantage of the accessibility features that mobile 
applications offer.
