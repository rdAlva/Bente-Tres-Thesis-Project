Abstract 
The study is a tool to identify the gap between what the educational institution produces 
and what the industry requires with respect to the assessment and satisfaction level of 
the graduates. The study was carried out to identify the employability of the BSA 
graduates of SPCF from 2014 to 2019. This also aimed to identify the relevance of the 
curriculum and study program, the practices at BSA and the competencies that assisted 
the graduates land a job and how they are beneficial in their jobs. 
There were 94 graduates that took part in the study out of the 103 graduates from 
2014-2019. Questionnaire was given to the respondents by students, and faculty staff of 
BSBA Department. The data collection was a combined effort of alumni, faculty staff, 
students and student leaders to the extent of using social media to reach out to the 
target respondents. The data collected were examined and interpreted using basic 
statistical tool as percentages and results were shown in tables. 
It was discovered in the study that 89.4% of the respondents obtained employment. 
Significantly, a large number of graduates acknowledged that curriculum, study program 
and practices at BSBA were useful in their jobs. It was also found that referral system is 
still a reliable and effective way of landing a job for the graduates. 
It was also found out that for the graduates to be more competitive and employable the 
following areas need to be improved as follows: entrepreneurial skills, communication 
skills, problem and decision making skills, language proficiency and information 
communication technologies (ICT) skills. It is strongly recommended also to formally 
establish linkage to industry partners in Angeles City and Pampanga. 
 
Chapter 1: Introduction 
SPCF envisions itself to become a leading and globally competitive educational 
institution in the fields of instruction, research and community service. The study 
informed the school authorities and its faculty and staff through knowing the present 
employability of its graduates at the BSA Department. 
The findings of the study were an important tool and reference material for the institution 
and its students and graduates. The study mapped out the path towards alignment of 

course offerings, developing strategies and methods that are suited to the needs of the 
industry given the ever-changing and highly volatile needs and requirements of the job 
market. This also been an eye-opener for the school authorities in the provision of 
needed resources and services for the students to be more competitive in an 
increasingly technological and global labor market competition. 
 
Chapter 2: Problem of the Study and Objectives 
What is the employability rate of SPCF BSA graduates of 2014-2019? This study aimed 
to provide facts and information meant to make the institution realize the employability 
rate of BSA graduates from 2014 to 2019. This study also determined the effectiveness 
of the BSA programs, curriculum and the methods as well as the whereabouts and the 
current situation of the graduates. The findings of this study can help the institution in its 
curriculum development and review to upgrade the knowledge skill and abilities needed 
by its students in line with requirements of the industry, or the labor market, thus 
ensuring the graduates of higher chance of employment. 
Objectives 
The study aimed to: 
a. determine the employment/unemployment rate of SPCF graduates; 
b. identify the reasons for graduates' unemployment; 
c. determine how long graduates could land a job after graduation; 
d. determine the sources for finding a job; 
e. find out the reasons for a graduates' staying on the job; 
f. identify the competencies learned in college, which graduates found very helpful; and 
g. determine which BA related activities they carried out in their jobs. 
 
Chapter 3: Research Methodology 
Sampling Plan 
The respondents of the study consisted mainly of SPCF, BSA graduates from 2014 to 
2019. 
Sampling Size 
There were 112 graduates from the BSA Department from 2014-2019 where all 
graduates were regarded as the research sample; hence, total enumeration was used.  

Research Instrument and Technique 
A survey questionnaire was created to gather important information for the study. The 
Questionnaire had been made in a way the respondents could easily understand what 
is being asked. 
Data Gathering Procedure 
The researcher sought the help of the school administration, the registrar's office, Office 
of external affairs, research and development office, Alumni Office, COB Dean's Office 
and Student organization who had built network or linkage with the graduates of the 
BSA Department. 
The faculty members of the BSA Department, the researcher sought cooperation of the 
students, who served as research enumerators for the study. The researcher had 
chosen currently enrolled students and members of the student organization of the 
department to help in the generation of needed data. 
Distribution of research instruments was given from the start of First Semester, School 
Year 2019-2020 upon pre-testing and validation of materials. The date of retrieval was 
at the end of August to come up with the final result and the presentation was before the 
end of the First Semester, SY 2019-2020. 
Statistical Treatment 
The data collected were tallied, tabulated, analyzed and treated statistically. Results 
were treated through frequency, percentages and shown using tables and pie charts. 
 
Chapter 4: Results and Discussion 
The intensive collection of data involved students who are taking research, feasibility 
studies and student leaders under the JPIA. The faculty members were also asked to 
track and re-establish connection and linkages with their previous students. The study 
also sought the help of alumni to support the gathering of data. 
There were 94 students who had responded on the study out of the 103 graduates from 
School Year 2014 to the end of SY 2019. It shows that 83.9% had responded on the 
survey. There were 28 males out of 33 who responded which is equivalent to 84.84% 
and 66 female out of 79 had responded which is 83.54%. 
Number of Graduates and Participants 
Gender: Male — Number of Graduates: 33 (29.36%), Number of Participants: 28 
(84.84%). Female — Number of Graduates: 79 (70.64%), Number of Participants: 66 
(83.54%). Total — Number of Graduates: 112 (110%), Number of Participants: 94 
(83.92%). 

A. Gender Distribution 
There were 94 students who responded on the questionnaire administered of which 28 
are male and 66 are female which is 29.8% and 70.2% respectively. This shows that 
there are more females than males who graduated at the department from 2011-2014. 
B. Employment Status of Graduates 
The table shows that 84 or 89.4% are currently working and 10 or 10.6 are unemployed 
or self-employed or waiting for time to go abroad. This shows that there is a high 
employment rate of graduates at the BSBA Department. 
C. Job Posting 
The respondents reported that 66% or 62 of the total respondents had stated that their 
current job is their first job and 32 of them which is 34% acknowledged that they are 
now on second job or more. 
D. Time Spent Before Landing a Job 
Most of the graduates stated that they get a job within 6 months which is 49 out of 94 
which is 52.1%. There were 28.7% respondents reported that they got the job within a 
year. There were 10.6% before two years after graduation. Only 5.3% reported that they 
have the job within one month after graduation and a small 3.2% or 3 graduates were 
able to get a job after two years of graduation. 
E. Source of Job 
There were 51.1% or 48 respondents reported that they get their job through referral 
from, SPCF, former professors, classmates and school mates, relatives and friends. 10 
or 10.6% stated that it is through job fairs. Walk in was reported by 17 or 18.1%. There 
were 4 graduates who stated that they land a job through agency and 15 or 16% used 
websites and search engines. 
F. Reason of Unemployment 
The 84 graduates or 89.4% stated not applicable since they are currently employed. It 
was found out that 3 or 3.2% stated that no connections, starting pay is low and lack of 
professional eligibility requirement was the cause for unemployment. Two graduates 
stated that family concern was the issue and two also stated that lack of experience 
prevents them from getting a job. Only one respondent stated that there is no job 
opportunity. 
G. Relevance of the Curriculum on the First Job 
There were 88 or 93.6% graduates stated that the curriculum is relevant to their first job. 
6.4% or 6 graduates believed that it was not relevant to their job of which 4 worked 
abroad as Seaman or Health Service Staff. 

H. Competencies in College that are Useful on their Job 
It was acknowledged that skills related to the course such as customer care, sales, 
retail job and customer relations are useful as reported by 40 or 42.6%. The problem 
solving skills and critical thinking skills came up with 26 respondents reporting or 27.7%. 
There were 15 or 16% of the graduates stated that human relation skills was useful 
followed by communication skills accounting for 9.6% and 4.3% reported 
entrepreneurial skills. 
I. BSBA Related Activities Practiced at Job 
It was confirmed by the respondents that marketing and sales topped the list for BA 
activities practiced at the job where 50% reported. 23.4% reported on customer 
relations. There were 17 or 18.1% stated that data gathering and research work was 
practiced. Office management and procedures, and human resource management were 
reported both at 4.3%. 
J. Location of the First Job 
Most of the graduates got a job in Angeles City and its vicinity including Clark Export 
Processing Zones of which 60 or 63.8% reported. There were 5 or 5.3% graduates 
working within Central Luzon and 22 or 23.4% within Pampanga. Three graduates 
stated they are working in Metro Manila and the remaining 4.3% reported they are 
working elsewhere including abroad.

Chapter 5: Conclusion and Recommendation 
It can be noted that from among the 94 respondents that accounts to 83% of the total 
BSBA graduates from 2011 to 2014, there were 89.4% or 84 graduates had obtained 
employment or currently working. Notably, 66% are still connected with their first job 
which explains that there is a high level of satisfaction on their current jobs. 
More than 52% reported that they land a job within six months after graduation and 
almost 30% got a job before one year. Hence, employability of the graduates was very 
high. It was acknowledged by the graduates that referral from people related from SPCF 
and to SPCF itself had greatly been the reason why they were employed. Job fair and 
walk in application was also widely acknowledged. 
The reason on unemployment still perceived by a few respondents as they lack 
connections and they lack experience and still maintained that there was no job 
opportunity. 
It was found out that the BSBA curriculum was acknowledged by 93.6% as relevant to 
their job. As they also stated that the competencies they obtained from BSBA were 
helpful in their job namely: customer care and customer relations, problem solving and 
decision making skills and communication skills. It also found out that activities gained 
from BSBA were carried out at their job like marketing activities, customer relations, 
data gathering and research, and community extension activities. 
Currently, most graduates are working in Angeles City and in Pampanga. The graduates 
acknowledge that they are highly adaptable in their jobs. It was also found out that 
graduates gained experiences from their current job and shared that their study 
program was relevant to their job. 
On the other hand, the following areas should be improved for the BSBA graduates to 
be more competitive and employable in the future, these are: entrepreneurial skills, 
communication skills, problem and decision making skills, language proficiency and ICT 
skills. It is further recommended that linkages to industries or companies within Angeles 
City and Pampanga should be established as they are the major labor market for BSBA 
Graduates.
