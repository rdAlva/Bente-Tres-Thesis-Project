Abstract 
The research aimed to determine the response of the SMEs in the Metro Clark Area on 
their marketing strategy and enterprise success using Social Media platforms as a 
business tool. Various activities were examined through correlation analysis to have a 
better understanding on social media's impact to the SMEs. The study had set a 
confidence level of 95% and a level of significance of 9% to determine the sample 
population of the study, Cronbach's Alpha was used in reliability scale. Simple random 
sampling was used in identifying the respondent. Likewise, convenience data gathering 
was employed based on accessibility, availability and cost effectiveness of gathering 
data. SPSS was utilized to analyze the data. 
The results had significantly shown that the availability of social media application ready 
devices did not create a barrier nor internet connectivity an obstacle; on the other hand 
a higher degree of participation in social media is encouraged. The promotion of social 
media for various marketing strategies and activities cannot be overlooked, hence, there 
should be a greater promotion and engagement. Create the right and a responsive 
awareness campaign that needs support from all sectors, primarily the government to 
encourage and realize the effect of social media in achieving enterprise success 
resulting in a better financial performance. The study concludes that a policy guideline 
aimed at protecting the SMEs from unethical and deceitful users of social media. 
Furthermore, an incentive scheme for SMEs needs to be studied to encourage usage of 
social media tools and applications for business endeavors. 
Keywords: social media, small and medium enterprises, marketing tool, marketing

Social Media also known as social networking is a phenomenon that changes the

Research Methodology 
The study will utilize descriptive research design to know the extent on the use of social 
media and its impact on marketing activities and SMEs growth in Metro Clark Area. The 
study will focus on SMEs that are registered business entities classified as small and 
medium enterprises. Simple random sampling will be utilized to derive the desired target 
respondents. Purposive sampling will be used to get easy access to the needed sample 
size. A structured questionnaire will be administered to owners and/or managers of the 
SMEs. Closed questions and open-ended questions will be utilized to assure reliable 
and complete data generated. 

Analysis of data will be done through SPSS (Statistical Package for Social Sciences) to 
calculate and determine descriptive statistics and correlation analysis. Descriptive 
statistical analysis will be generated and data will be tabulated using percentages for 
the non-numeric information gathered where the findings were summarized, explained 
and interpreted.
