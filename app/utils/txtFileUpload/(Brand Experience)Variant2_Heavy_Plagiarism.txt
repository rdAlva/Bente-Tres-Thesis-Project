Brand Experience to Consumer Purchase Intention of Baby Charm Marquee Mall Branch 
 
Abstract 
The study examined the Relationship of Brand Experience to Customer Purchase Intention 
at Baby Charm Marquee Mall Branch. The research determines if Brand Experience and 
Customer Purchasing Intention have a powerful relationship based on Sensory, Affective, 
Behavioral and Intellectual. Mean rating table was used to rate the answers of 292 
respondents on the self-made questionnaire given out on the actual survey. The results 
were obtained using mean rating and Pearson R Correlation as a statistical tool. The results 
showed that all four variables related to brand experience namely Sensory, Affective, 
Behavioral and Intellectual all received a rating of strongly agree. Therefore, the 
researchers conclude that brand experience has a strong positive correlation to consumer 
purchase intention. The researchers suggest that Baby Charm should enhance its Brand 
Experience as it affects Consumer Purchase Intention. Based on the results shown that 
Factors of Brand Experience has a correlation to Consumer Purchase Intention. 
(Keywords: Brand Experience, Sensory, Affective, Behavioral, Intellectual, Consumer Purchase 
Intention)

Methods 
Research Design 
This research will use descriptive method to describe information that will help understand 
the relationship of the four factors, which are sensory, affective, intellectual and behavioral 
and to their consumer purchase intention. The descriptive method is structured in the form 
of a survey and data collection instruments are designed to extract experiences from 
predetermined response classifications. Quantitative strategies emphasize objective 
measurements and the statistical, scientific, or numerical examination of information 
collected through surveys, questionnaires, and studies, or by manipulating pre-existing 
measurable information using computational techniques. Correlational research could be a 
type of non-experimental research method, in which a researcher measures two variables, 
understands and assesses the statistical relationship between them with no effect from any 
outside variable. The respondents’ survey results will be summarized, compared, and 
generalized. The researchers wish to find out consistency, accuracy and reliability of data 
collected from the survey.

A mother’s love needs to be given unconditionally to establish trust and a strong foundation

confidence interval calculation and minimize margin of errors. Using Raosoft, it 
automatically computes the total number of respondents showing 292 respondents for the

target market. 
Research Instrument 
The research instrument that has been used in this research paper was a self-made 
questionnaire. This was the instrument used as a tool to identify how brand experience 
factors such as sensory, affective, behavioral, and intellectual impact consumer purchase 
intention. The researchers used the Likert scale described thus: Strongly Disagree -1, 
Disagree – 2, Agree – 3 and Strongly Agree – 4.

questionnaire before conducting the actual survey.

integrates an all-encompassing set of conditions created by a company to influence the 
feeling of a customer about a specific product or company name. Through a combination of 
different modes, a buyer works to interact with a brand, companies try to form a common

Definition of Terms 
Brand – a type of product manufactured by a particular company under a particular name. 
It is also the identity of the product for consumers to be familiarized with it. 
Experience – practical contact with and observation of facts or events. 
Sensory – relating to sensation or the physical senses transmitted or perceived by the 
senses. 
Affective – describes something that has been influenced by emotions, is a result of 
emotions, or expresses emotion. 
Intellectual – means involving a person’s ability to think and to understand ideas and 
information. 
Behavioural – an action that is observable and measurable. 
Purchase intention – the preference of consumer to buy the product or service. In other 
words, another aspect that the consumer will purchase a product after evaluation.
