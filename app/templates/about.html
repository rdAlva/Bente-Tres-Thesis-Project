About HAUdemicCheck

A web-based academic document similarity detection system for Holy Angel University.

About HAUdemicCheck

HAUdemicCheck is a web-based academic document similarity detection system developed to assist instructors in identifying textual similarities among academic documents. It applies hybrid Natural Language Processing techniques to compare submitted documents with an institutional reference repository and provide similarity results for instructor review.

Purpose of the System

HAUdemicCheck was developed to support instructors in reviewing academic submissions by providing similarity measurements and identifying potentially similar passages. The system is designed to assist the instructor's evaluation process by presenting evidence of textual similarity rather than making an automatic determination of plagiarism.

How It Works

The analysis pipeline follows seven stages, from document submission to sentence-level review.

  1. 1

    Upload Document

    The instructor submits an academic document for analysis.

  2. 2

    Text Extraction

    Text is extracted from the submitted document.

  3. 3

    Text Preprocessing

    The extracted text is prepared for NLP-based comparison.

  4. 4

    Similarity Analysis

    The document is compared with documents in the institutional repository using multiple similarity measures.

  5. 5

    Threshold Evaluation

    The combined similarity score is evaluated against the configured threshold.

  6. 6

    Promotion or Review

    Documents below the threshold are promoted, while documents meeting or exceeding the threshold are sent for instructor review.

  7. 7

    Sentence-Level Review

    Flagged documents can be examined at the sentence level, with similar passages visually highlighted.

Core Technologies

TF-IDF

Measures the importance of terms within documents and represents documents as weighted numerical vectors.

Cosine Similarity

Measures the overall similarity between document representations based on their TF-IDF vectors.

N-grams

Captures recurring local word and phrase patterns using unigrams, bigrams, and trigrams.

Jaccard Similarity

Measures the overlap between shared N-gram sets across documents.

Configurable Threshold

Allows the instructor to control the similarity decision boundary used by the system.

Sentence-Level Matching

Identifies potentially similar sentence pairs and supports visual highlighting during review.
