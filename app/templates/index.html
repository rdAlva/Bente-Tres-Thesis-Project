Similarity Algorithm Overview

Combined Similarity Score
Scombined = (C + J1 + J2 + J3) / 4
C
TF-IDF Cosine Similarity
J1
Unigram Jaccard Similarity
J2
Bigram Jaccard Similarity
J3
Trigram Jaccard Similarity

Cosine Similarity looks at how similar the overall content and important terms are between two documents, while Jaccard Similarity looks at how much their words and word combinations overlap. Using both helps the system detect documents that are similar in meaning as well as documents that share the same or very similar wording.

The system calculates the final document similarity score by averaging the TF-IDF cosine similarity and the three Jaccard similarity scores.

Decision Rule
If: Scombined ≥ Threshold → WITHHOLD / REVIEW
If: Scombined < Threshold → PROMOTE
