Dear School Heads: 
Warm greetings! 
It is with great pleasure that we invite your institution to participate in the 30th Word Cup 
Philippines National Journalism Conference and Contests, to be held on 9-11 October 2026 in 
Baguio City. 
This milestone edition reaffirms Word Cup's commitment to experiential journalism. What has 
always set Word Cup apart is not merely that participants compete, but that they report under real 
conditions. While many journalism workshops simulate the newsroom, Word Cup places 
participants where stories actually unfold —through live press conferences, coverage of an actual 
sporting event, and national-level publication opportunities and recognition that cultivate accuracy, 
critical thinking, ethical judgment, and responsible storytelling.  
Participants will engage in: 
 
Learning sessions with media practitioners, and industry experts; 
 
A live press conference where participants gather information, ask questions, and produce 
news stories under newsroom conditions; 
 
Coverage of an actual kickboxing event to strengthen sports journalism skills through 
firsthand observation and reporting; and 
 
Journalism contests that challenge participants' creativity, accuracy, and journalistic 
excellence. 
In today's rapidly evolving media landscape, the ability to verify facts, think critically, and report 
responsibly has never been more important. More than a competition, the 30th Word Cup equips 
delegates with these essential competencies by allowing them to experience journalism as it is 
practiced. 
We warmly invite you to send a delegation of campus journalists and publication advisers to this 
milestone gathering. Details on registration, the program, contest guidelines, and venue are 
attached.

DETAILS OF REGISTRATION 
Objectives of the press coverage and national contest: 
1. To strengthen the competencies of student journalists and school paper advisers through hands-on 
capacity-building sessions aligned with current media practices and digital platforms. 
2. To cultivate critical thinking, collaboration, and newsroom decision-making skills through authentic 
newsroom and press conference simulations. 
3. To encourage the production of high-quality journalistic outputs that highlight community issues, 
development concerns, and responsible storytelling. 
4. To recognize and promote excellence in campus journalism through institutional recognition such 
as the Word Cup Ambassadors Award for exemplary schools, advisers, and education leaders. 
 
Contests are optional and open to: 
● 
Elementary, junior, and senior high school students 
● 
Tertiary students 
● 
School paper advisers, language and communication arts teachers, department heads, school 
heads, and  information officers 
 
What to prepare: 
● 
Mobile Journalism participants – cellphone, lapel/mic, and/or laptop with video editing applications 
● 
Photojournalism participants – cameras or cellphones
