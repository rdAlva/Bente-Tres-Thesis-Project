 
 
 
 
 
 
24 August 2026 
 
 
To All Concerned Higher Education Institutions 
59th Search for the Ten Outstanding Students of the Philippines 
 
 
Dear Presidents, Deans, Directors, and TOSP Coordinators, 
 
Warm greetings from TOSP-R3ACH and the Ten Outstanding Students of the Philippines (TOSP) 
Regional Executive Committee! 
We respectfully inform you that, after careful consideration of the schedule of the Licensure 
Examination for Teachers, which coincides with the original dates of the TOSP Regional Week, the 5-
day program will be moved from 17 - 21 September to 24 - 28 September. 
The updated schedule of activities is as follows: 
24 September – Courtesy Visit at CHEDRO III Office and Mekeni Corporation | Pampanga 
25 September – Regional Formation Program Day 1 | Bataan 
26 September – Regional Formation Program Day 2 | Bataan 
27 September – Province and Community Day | Bataan 
28 September – Regional Interview, Judging, and Awarding | Bataan 
We kindly request your assistance in disseminating this schedule adjustment to your respective finalists 
and concerned offices. We sincerely appreciate your understanding, support, and flexibility regarding 
this change. 
Should you have any questions or require further clarification, please feel free to contact us via email 
or through Mark Angelo Arceo, TOSP-R3ACH Partnerships and External Affairs Committee 
Associate Chair, at 0915-580-7500. 
Thank you for your continued guidance and support for your finalists throughout this TOSP journey. 
We look forward to working with you in celebrating excellence, leadership, and service among the 
youth. 
 
Lagi’t lagi, para sa bayan, 
 
 
 
Mario L. Cuengco, Jr., MM, RMP, CCREP 
Member, TOSP Regional Executive Committee
