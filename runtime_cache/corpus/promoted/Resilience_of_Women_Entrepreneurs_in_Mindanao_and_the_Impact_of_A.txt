1.8. Impact of Digital: Greater Diversification,
Spreading of Risk, Access to Markets
While the digital landscape for women nano
entrepreneurs in Northern Mindanao is improving,
challenges remain in digital literacy, connectivity, and
affordability. Digital adopters continue to leverage
technology to diversify their income and expand market
access, but non-digital adopters struggle due to limited
resources and knowledge.
Digital adopters can access broader markets through
social media and e-commerce platforms, like Facebook,
Shopee, and Lazada. This broader access allows them
to engage in multiple income-generating activities,
such as aquaculture and fishing, retail operations, crafts,
and service delivery. By leveraging digital platforms,
these women can efficiently reach a wider customer
base. This strategic approach to market access enables
digital adopters to reduce their vulnerability to
economic shocks.
Digital tools also facilitate better risk management by
providing alternative avenues for sales and sourcing
supplies. Women nano-entrepreneurs who adopted
digital platforms diversified their supply chains and
marketing efforts, ensuring that their businesses
remain resilient. While the economy's reopening saw
a reduction in online selling and sourcing supplies
for income-generating activities, digital adopters still
maintained diversified portfolios that allowed them to
withstand market fluctuations. Their diversified income
streams and access to broader markets have improved
their economic resilience, making them less reliant on
traditional labor-intensive industries.
Furthermore, digital adoption has increased efficiency
in customer engagement and financial management.
Although challenges such as weak signal, high costs,
and limited digital literacy persist, women who use
digital tools can showcase their products better, enhance
communication, and access customers from further away.
However, as people return to physical stores, some
women reported a decline in digital tool usage.

From March 2020 to 2023, the study revealed that
women nano-entrepreneurs faced continuous economic
shocks due to the pandemic and natural disasters such
as typhoons, floods, and earthquakes. Women nano-
entrepreneurs demonstrated remarkable resilience
through various coping mechanisms. These included
withdrawing savings, seeking aid from government
and politicians, accessing loans from MFIs, finding
additional work or income, and relying on family
assistance. The FGDs underscored the effectiveness
of strategies like savings withdrawal and community-
based insurance practices such as damayan/dayong.
However, loans were less favored due to the financial
burden they imposed. This emphasizes the need for
immediate financial access and robust community
support systems to manage the economic impacts of
disasters effectively.
The report offers several recommendations aimed at
improving the resilience and economic empowerment
of women nano-entrepreneurs in Mindanao, particularly
in the face of external shocks such as natural
disasters and economic crises. These include gender-
responsive programs that support and enhance the
efforts of Filipino women nano-entrepreneurs by
ensuring they are not disproportionately burdened
by economic and household responsibilities. Public
daycare centers should be adequately staffed to free
up time for mothers to focus on their businesses.
Comprehensive, fast, and affordable insurance solutions
should be developed by MFIs, and the Insurance
Commission should create an enabling environment
for microinsurance to thrive. Government support and
social insurance programs need to lower enrollment
barriers, expand coverage to include disasters, and
streamline aid distribution processes. Digital finance
literacy and access should be improved by investing
in digital infrastructure, providing tailored training
programs, and delivering financial literacy modules
online. Disaster mitigation strategies should include
infrastructure improvements and long-term micro
housing loans facilitated by MFIs.
By implementing these policy recommendations, the
resilience and economic empowerment of women
nano-entrepreneurs in Mindanao can be significantly
enhanced, ensuring they have the tools and resources
necessary to thrive in the face of external shocks.
