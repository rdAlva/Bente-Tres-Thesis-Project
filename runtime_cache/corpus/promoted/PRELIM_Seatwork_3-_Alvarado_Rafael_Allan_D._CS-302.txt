Based on your Software Engineering project proposal, provide information on the following: 
1. Describe in detail 3-5 main functions that your proposed system must perform. 
Vendwise is our proposed system, which includes three main functions: a Point of Sale (POS) 
system, an inventory management module, and a sales reporting dashboard. Firstly, the POS 
system enables users to record transactions more effectively than with traditional manual methods. 
This ensures that every transaction is accurately accounted for, enhancing the efficiency of the 
business's sales processing. Secondly, the inventory management module allows users to track 
their inventory more efficiently, facilitating better management of stock levels for their products. 
It also features alerts for products that have low stock levels. Lastly, the sales reporting dashboard 
provides users with a summary of their sales on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. This 
information is presented in graphical charts, enabling users to identify their best-selling products, 
observe sales trends, and calculate total revenue. This proposed application will be mobile-
friendly and include offline functionality for added convenience. 
1. List 3-5 different types of users (admin, end-user, students, customers, etc.) and their expertise 
level. Describe in detail how they will interact with the system. 
 
The administrator possesses a high level of skill, as they are responsible for managing system 
settings, user accounts, and overall security. The store manager possesses a moderate level of 
expertise with the system, as they use it to monitor sales and manage inventory. The cashier or 
front desk staff have a low to mid-level of expertise, as their primary tasks include taking orders, 
processing transactions, and applying discounts. 
 
2. Describe the hardware, software, and network environment required to run the system. 
The Vendwise Application is specifically designed for mobile devices, ensuring both accessibility 
and mobility. It is compatible with most devices, particularly those in the low to mid-range 
categories. The application's front end is developed using Flutter, while the back end is built with 
Java. For data storage, it utilizes SQLite. A key feature of the Vendwise Application is its offline 
usability. We recognize that network connectivity can be unreliable, so this feature allows users to 
operate the app without an internet connection. This means that even if the network goes down, 
you can continue to use the application without worrying about disruptions to your business.
