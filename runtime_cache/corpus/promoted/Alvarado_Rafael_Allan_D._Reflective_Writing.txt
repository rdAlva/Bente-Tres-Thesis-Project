AI is not the “NEW Human.” 
 
In the current era of advanced technologies, we tend to take the easy paths that technologies 
provide for us. When we write simple essays, some use artificial intelligence (AI) to make them. By doing 
this we are not using our humane way of thinking as AI is not human, AI is a machine. For us to use AI in 
creating simple tasks, we tend to forget that we as humans can do much better than the ones AI makes.  
 
“True intellect is capable of moral thought (Chomsky et al., 2023).”. What makes us capable of 
being able to call ourselves human, is the ability to have moral thought. Having moral and ethical 
principles makes us limit ourselves on what we say or do. While AI does not have this property as it is a 
machine made by humans. It only gives on whatever it can generate without thinking if it is morally right 
or wrong. This is one of the challenges programmers of AI technology continue to face because how can 
you teach a machine to think like a human? Human beings are essentially complex beings.  Human beings 
are the ones who I believe possess true intelligence because we follow our moral beliefs and the ethical 
principles of society. 
 
Chomsky stated that the human mind is different from ChatGPT and other similar AI 
technologies. ChatGPT and other AI technologies work by finding the right pattern of data in their 
massive storage to generate answers. Unlike humans, which require a minimal amount of data to generate 
complex thoughts. Human minds can express complex emotions that help them navigate complex social 
interactions. Artificial intelligence (AI) or ChatGPT cannot express emotions as humans do. This is 
because AI cannot make decisions on its own and is dependent on the instructions programmed by 
humans. As an unconscious machine, AI still relies on humans to operate effectively. The human mind 
possesses creativity, and holistic understanding unlike how AI is programmed to perform specific tasks 
(Quarks, 2023). 
 
Machine learning is capable of learning almost anything, but it cannot distinguish between what 
is possible and what is impossible (Chomsky et al., 2023). AI makes things overly complete with deep 
words that humans barely use when you ask it to do certain tasks. It does not mind plagiarizing other 
people's works as it is a machine, not a human. It does not have thoughts of its own. It moves based on 
what data a user puts in. Humans and machines have different ways of learning. Humans learn through 
direct or shared experience, while machines learn through previous data. As I said in my first realization, 
Machines do not follow any ethical principles or even moral principles that we humans follow in our 
everyday lives. 
 

In conclusion, the advances in AI technology in this era are still not enough to replace humans. 
Programmers of AI are still blocked by how machines are unable to be taught of moral and ethical 
principles we humans use. The human mind has its creativity, understanding, emotions, and complex 
thoughts that we have been developing since our birth in this world. This implies that AI still has a lot 
more food to eat for it to develop and think like us. It makes some tasks overly complicated that it cannot 
determine what is right or wrong as it doesn’t have any thoughts of its own. AI is a machine that is still 
dependent on humans to operate correctly. It does not mind plagiarizing other people's work because AI 
technology generates its answers from a wide range of information from its database. AI does not follow 
any ethical or moral principles.
