Yes, based on what Fidel V. Ramos proclaimed was done in Manila City on 
October 7, 1994. "Filipino Values Month" was declared every year in the month of 
November. It is said that the observance celebrates each person's triumph by 
overcoming all the obstacles that come in our way and to create a world where we 
rejoice uniquely as one with Filipino values. Through what the president declared, we 
have the chance to celebrate Filipino values while we respect each other's perspectives 
through online learning and activities in either public or private schools.
