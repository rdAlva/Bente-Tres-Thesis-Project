Computer science is a field that primarily focuses on the development and testing of software, as 
well as software systems. (Michigan Technological University, 2023). However, a computer science 
professional needs to be proficient in communication skills, not just coding. When presenting their codes 
to clients, programmers need to provide vital information effectively. Communication is a crucial skill for 
IT professionals since they need to present data or propose projects to their superiors or clients 
(Backlinkworks, 2023). It is a skill that helps build relationships between individuals.  
 
Communication is a complex and dynamic process that is influenced by various factors, including 
the sender's encoding of the message and any communication barriers that may exist. As new information 
or perspectives are introduced, the process can change over time (Libretexts, 2022). Schramm's model is 
an example of complex communication that emphasizes the importance of personal experiences, attitudes, 
values, and beliefs in the communication process. These factors greatly influence how messages are sent 
from the source and received by the recipient, as well as how they are interpreted and given meaning. The 
model also expands on the process of encoding and decoding the message and highlights the importance 
of two-way communication in a circular format (Schramm’s Model of Communication, n.d.)
