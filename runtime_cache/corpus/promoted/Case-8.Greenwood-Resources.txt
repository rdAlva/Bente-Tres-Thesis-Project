Statement of the Problem 
 
Background of the Case 
 
Greenwood Resources was found in 1998 by Jeff Nuss and is molded a niche in the 
market by combining varietal forestry with intensive plantation management. Jeff 
Nuss’s vision was to create a product that would last a lifetime; his experience in bio 
resources gave him the tools and knowledge to choose a product that would generate a 
competitive advantage. No other company was investing in sustainable forestry that’s 
why he took the chance and firmly believed the market for sustainable forestry would 
one day flourish. Additionally, Jeff analyzed the market and knew how pricey and time- 
consuming sustainable forestry was, he knew nobody was going to invest in time or 
money for this industry. According to Dess et al (2018), demand conditions are products 
that are demanded by consumers driving other companies to create or improve 
innovative products, advance products, or services. 
 
Greenwood Resources is a leading environmental tree plantation industry that 
specializes in asset management through high yield tree plantations. Greenwood 
struggled for over 10 years to generate high profits throughout its first years of 
operation. An investment opportunity opened doors for the company, and they 
established Green China Forestry Company. 
 
The International Strategy of Greenwood Resources is to advance their products to 
appeal to international markets. China had a high yield potential for poplar tree farming. 
Therefore, excessive logging and deforestation made the tree industry more appealing 
to countries that were struggling with the demand in trees.

Alternative courses of Action 
 
Solution 1: Strategic Management  
  
Advantage: In strategic management, it includes the strategies of every division and a 
strategy as a whole. Because critical decision making is crucial, in such manner, they 
would know if it is recommendable for them to invest in one of both tasks. 
 
Disadvantage: They might lose the opportunity to expand and attract capital investors 
for not investing at the big poplar plantation. 
 
 

 
Solution 2: Compliance to legal documents 
 
Advantage: Compliance with laws to the land and forest resources are clearly expound 
and lawfully established. 
 
Disadvantage: If this happens, the managerial issue may arise and might cause hassle 
and disruption to the company.  
 
Solution 3: Work on capital investment 
   
Advantage: Procurement of fund to sustain finances and avoids insufficiency moreover 
it helps the company to estimate which investment would yield the possible return. 
  
Disadvantage: The company’s operating cash flow might not be enough to cover 
anticipated costs. The company might resort to outside financing to cover up the 
internal shortfall. 
 
Recommendation 
 
With the above analysis, we, therefore, recommend alternative courses of action 
numbers 1 and 3. 
 
These two actions propose strategic management and work on capital investment. 
Since thorough decision making must be made among the CEO and top management, 
for them to effectively ensure their sustainability. In addition, working on capital 
investment is essential for the company to operate simultaneously with no insufficiency 
of funds.

The mission of Greenwood Conservancy is to establish a refuge that will focus

The refuge will ensure the protection of indigenous wildlife, waterfowl, 
songbirds, and birds of prey; and will encourage the growth of native trees, plants and 
wildflowers. 
 
It will permanently protect a large tract of land from development and will serve
