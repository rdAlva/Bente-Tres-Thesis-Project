Introduction
As the current pandemic situation still occurs to this day, it is
significant that humans must not forget about taking care of the
environment despite the difficulty and hardships that the majority of
people are experiencing. This can be done by reducing its waste and
having
sufficient
awareness
of
other things that contribute to the
environmental problem. According to the University of Colorado Boulder,
based on a study done by USA Today, the average American adult
spends $1,497 a month on nonessential items. In this study, it confirms that
if netizens from other countries have the habit of wasting their money on
unnecessary items, this can be also applied to the other people from
different countries as well
due to the reason “this adds up extremely
quickly and there are about 230 million tons of municipal garbage
produced each year. “ This is evidence that if humans do not have
enough knowledge about how to conserve their materials and resources,
it will lead to millions of garbage that will be produced even in the future
generations.

There are a manageable number of ways on how to prevent and to
lessen the waste. This is composting, utilizing reusable products, and recycling.
The GreenWorks team named this “C.U.R.E.” because this will help cure the
wounds of the environment.
Despite the pandemic that millions of people are facing, the activities
that GreenWorks company implemented are manageable and every person of
all ages could easily handle the plans. Furthermore, the team also conducted
an experiment on how people can manage to do these if the covid-19 and
other mutated viruses are relevant today. GreenWorks came to the conclusion
that all the content, news, updates, and incoming plans regarding the proposal
will be posted in the facebook page that the company created real time. In this
method, people do not need to go out to execute these action plans because
the team is confident that these can be done inside the house or even in their
backyard.

Additionally, humans already used minerals but not to such an extent that
they will be used in everyday life. In the present era, the majority of people
heavily relied on minerals and other natural resources without knowing the
actual effects and consequences. The industrial world consumes millions of
minerals without replacing and conserving them, hence, the present and mostly
the future generations will suffer because nature takes hundreds of years to
millions of years to produce mineral deposits.
The company also added an additional plan regarding the mineral
resources because this plays a vital role in daily lives of people, moreover,
almost all the objects, infrastructure, materials that surrounds humans are made
up of minerals. In this sense, it is the responsibility of people to gather adequate
knowledge regarding the minerals surrounding them. Spreading an informative
brochure of its importance and why people should value its existence will give
awareness to put emphasis on minerals resources. To save paper, and to
prevent paper pollution, the brochure will be posted online, or each family can
only have 1-2 brochures.

II.
Purpose
The misbehavior of humans causes major changes, and people were
already experiencing the unpleasant effects through the community they live in,
as a result, the team wants to convey knowledge and alertness regarding waste
that harm the environment and other living things, to give awareness how
crucial practicing the use of natural resources and materials is, to have better
insights about how to significantly minimize waste, to obtain the understanding
concerning the importance of minerals in our daily lives, and to support the
community by offering realistic activities that would result in a healthy and green
environment.
The purpose of this project is not only about giving a better environment,
but also to make the people stay inside their homes to prevent the increasing
number of covid cases in the country, and while being at home, the project
aims to provide information and more knowledge about minerals resources and
to motivate the people of the community to read through the brochure that the
company created. This will teach them reading comprehension, and also to
have an active body by doing the “C.U.R.E” activities because this can also
serve as their exercise.

III.
Project Description
The project plans to educate people on how to properly use minerals
and to properly dispose of the wastes. It will also share knowledge on how
to keep the environment healthy.
A. Goals and Objectives/Research Questions
With the increase in population, mineral consumption and waste have
been steadily increasing. People without knowledge inappropriately use
minerals and help with the increase in pollution. The goal of the project is
to give awareness on how to properly use minerals, this will also teach
people how to minimize waste by applying the appropriate activities such
as recycling, utilizing reusable products, and composting or “C.U.R.E.” As
the group practices green methods, the project will help keep the
environment green and clean and shape the minds of the younger
generation. The group’s objectives are the following:
●
To give awareness about mineral consumption
●
Conservation of mineral resources
●
To teach proper waste management

B. Methodology and Timelines
The company is expecting to have its schedule based on the timeline
below to implement the plans for each week. However, due to the

better results at the end of the project gives motivation to pursue the plan.

proposal gives hope for the company to persistently offer its best services
to humankind.

C. Benefits/Anticipated Outcomes
The company will give brochures, and in this way, people will be motivated to
join in. The anticipated outcome is that many people will participate in this
project proposal. A brochure will be given to them via online or in person, in this
way, motivation will come to them and realize how important the proposal is for
them and to the environment as well. The crew will ensure that all of the action
plans will be carried out appropriately by being active and having desirable
characteristics that could strengthen the project. The company also expects to
achieve a greener and healthy environment, and also to shape the minds of
the people to have a more detailed understanding of waste, and topics under
the minerals. This will be also beneficial to the health of the majority of people
around the society, because this can serve as their daily exercise that could
help their own body. This will open their minds that helping reduce the waste
and having adequate background information about minerals would result in a
better place that they have always dreamt of. To wake up the consciousness of
every person to start doing the “C.U.R.E” campaign, a project proposal like this
would be beneficial to society and nature as it gives a good reason to begin an
action and not just by words.
