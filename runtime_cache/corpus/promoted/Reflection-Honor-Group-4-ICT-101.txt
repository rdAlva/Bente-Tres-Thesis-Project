SHORT STORY
Once upon a time in a certain peaceful village, there exists a young man named Kris.
He was well known and recognized for his golden heart, his generosity, and his
hardworking mannerisms and personality. He most certainly believed that he had
inherited these virtuous aspects and mannerisms from his parents. His father, named
Dan, was a man of imagination and creativity. His mother, Ester, on the other hand,
was equally as hardworking and kind hearted, her caring nature a notable feat.
On one Monday morning, Kris' parents requested and asked for a favor to take care
and responsibility of the household. He was asked to handle his baby nephew, as
well as to manage the chores and tasks, as his father will be out to worm for the
whole day, while his mother will be spending the time out with her friends. They
needed someone to help keep the house clean, and most importantly, keep the
nephew safe and treated. Kris didn't hesitate to volunteer and accept the request of
his parents because he knew that they had sacrificed themselves and took on many
workloads to support him and his future. Hence, why he didn't hesitate to repay the
debt of the handworks of his parents.
So, he let his parents off and took care of his responsibility. He let his mother enjoy
her day off, and his father to rest once he came home after his shift from work. When
Kris' parents came home some time after dark, they were not only relieved but also
thankful and deeply moved by his commitment to their favor. They knew their child
was one of virtuosity. Once home, and seeing their child and nephew well treated
and the house well kept, the parents said. "You have truly matured and grown to be
a good man. Your willingness to help us and your obedience not only bring honour to
us as your parents— but also brings honour and spotlight to your virtuous self. " Kris,
who smiled at his parents' words, replied. "I never nor will I ever have plans to reject
your favours and requests for help, because you have already done so much to help
and support me— and that, I am thankful for. I can only return your graciousness,
and your love in return by reciprocating it twice as much. "
MORAL OF THE STORY: Respect your parents, honour their guidance, and cherish
their unwavering love and sacrifice. They have dedicated their lives into making their
children live a brighter future, and gave them opportunities to do so many things.
Follow their lead and guidance, and you will certainly find success and fulfilment.
