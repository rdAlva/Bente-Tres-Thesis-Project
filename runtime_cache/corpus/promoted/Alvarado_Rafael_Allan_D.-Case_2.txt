Question 
1. Your friend Jack confides to you that he has a drug problem. He begs you not to tell anyone. He 
says he needs someone in whom to confide. He does not plan to stop using drugs but wants you 
to know in case things go badly for him. What are possible courses of action for you? What are 
the advantages and disadvantages of each one? 
 
My first course of action would be to persuade him to stop using illegal substances which would 
affect his health and life afterwards. Since being involved in such illegal things can result in him being in 
prison. I feel like he still wants his life since he confides to me his secret. I would persuade him to go to a 
drug reform center to try and help him change his life and fate of being in prison. I hope he can change 
his way of living for himself and his family. If I cannot persuade him to stop using illegal substances, then 
my next course of action would be to tell the police of his wrongdoings. Now the question is why I would 
turn a friend of mine to the police, it is because the things he has done are not ethically and morally 
correct. Yes, he is a friend of mine, but I will not tolerate something outside my virtues. For the 
advantages of my course of action, I would like for my friends to see that I care for their well-being and 
that I do not want my friends to choose a path that would lead to their downfall or even result in their 
deaths. I will help when they need help, but I will not tolerate or help them do any wrongdoings outside 
of my virtues and morals even if he/she is a friend of mine. The disadvantages of my action would be that 
I would lose his trust or even worse he breaks our friendship. He might even threaten me if I keep on 
persuading him to change his ways. I hope that nothing bad happens to him and I hope that he can change 
his ways.
